# Voice and tone — Meu

Write as a calm operator describing account state, configuration and the next useful action. Charcoal-canvas green/gold Graphite and warm forest/apricot Pearl do not call for promotional copy.

- Use sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and state text. Keep standard acronyms such as API, LLM and PnL.
- Use concrete actions: “View agents”, “Refresh portfolio”, “Connect exchange”. Avoid hype and performance promises.
- Distinguish empty from unavailable: “No open positions” and “Positions are unavailable. Refresh to retry.” describe different states.
- State the relevant cause and recovery when known. Never invent a diagnosis or expose credential values.
- Preserve actual exchange currency and units. Signed PnL, monetary totals and precise quantities must match the data; show an em dash when unavailable.
- Label sample data as illustrative. “Daily volume of available fills” must not be rewritten as returns, profit or account equity.
- Do not present future crypto research, chat control, unsupported bracket behavior or notification channels as available.
- Use descriptive state labels and icons with semantic success, warning and error colors. Do not require uppercase terminal styling to communicate status.

Follow the current matte design rules in `../../../DESIGN.md` from the design-system root. Older terminal and purple/pink brand descriptions in archived artifacts are historical.
